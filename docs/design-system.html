06Usage Rules

Do

  • Use orange for one main action or highlight per area
  • Keep role colors tied to their role everywhere
  • Use condensed caps for labels and plain Barlow for sentences
  • Keep text light (#f0f0f0 / #c9d3dd) on dark surfaces

Don't

  • Put orange text on a yellow (SLAM) background
  • Use role colors for decoration with no meaning
  • Set long paragraphs in Barlow Condensed
  • Use Amazon logos; the wordmark is text only
